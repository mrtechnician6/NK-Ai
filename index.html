✦ Our Story

More Than a Café.
A Sanctuary.

Nestled in the heart of Kapan, Budhanilkantha, Chiya Lighter was born from a simple belief — that the best conversations happen over the perfect cup. Whether you crave the warmth of masala chiya, the depth of single-origin coffee, or the calm of an evening hookah session, we have crafted a space for you.
